Still winless

Azzfaulters are 0-7 after the fourth-highest score of the week

They put up 132.18 and lost to Bomb Squad by 6.14. Joe Burrow had 28.88 and Brandon Aubrey added 14.0. Two of their seven losses have come by fewer than 7 points, and their scoring says they should have about 2.6 wins.

Skid

Winning streak

kcnotbuns win a third straight on Jaxon Smith-Njigba's 31.3

The wide receiver had the highest score by any starter this week, and kcnotbuns beat Kiffin's Kittens 124.92 to 110.6. They are 4-3 and sit 7th in the standings, just outside the top six.

Defense

Two defenses finished in the red

Make Bama Great Again!'s Baltimore defense scored -3.0 and Kiffin's Kittens' New England defense scored -1.0. Both teams lost. Make Bama also had only 5.3 points on the bench, the league's lowest.

Scoring

Week 7 averaged 126.5 per team, with the highest floor of the season

JoshisAntifa's 109.68 was the lowest score of the week, yet it is the best low of any week this season. Week 6 averaged 118.2. Five teams topped 130.

Bubble

Five teams are 4-3 and the top six is a points-for tiebreak

Hanginwithhernandez, Kittens, Bomb Squad, Beggars and kcnotbuns all sit at 4-3, behind 5-2 JoshisAntifa and Club Space. Only kcnotbuns miss the cut on points for, and Picken Good Bijana (3-4) are one game back.

Beggars were 3-3 two weeks ago and have since scored 131.04 and 148.46 to climb to 4-3. The simulation now gives them 73% to make the playoffs, up from 65%, and nobody was louder this week than their 38.78-point win.

Kittens, check the defense

Kiffin's Kittens lost by 14.32 with Nico Collins at 2.6, Patrick Mahomes at 6.0 and the New England defense at -1.0. They left 59.7 points on the bench, and their playoff odds slid from 99% to 91%.

Peaks and valleys
